    12. Diff implementations, algorithms and compiler tuning
    UID: BENCH-012
    STATEMENT:

    Compare GNU diff default/minimal/speed-large-files, same-source generic/native GNU and Rust uutils builds, official uutils, BusyBox and Git Myers/minimal/patience/histogram. Separate compiler experiments from distribution comparisons.
