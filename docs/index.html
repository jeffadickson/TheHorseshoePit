Hockey Analysis

HockeyBalance reports for SCAHA schedules.

Reports

  • 10U B Regular Season — league-wide report
  • 10U B Flyers (2) — team report

What is included?

Each report includes filterable and sortable tables for player goals, player points, team balance, team standings, player totals, and goal reconciliation.

The Goals table includes an Include assists button. Team balance lists the top three scorers with goals:assists:points.
